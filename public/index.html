Our Heritage & Craft

The Story Behind Vaidya

Rooted in age-old traditional wellness and elevated by modern sustainable extraction, Vaidya was founded on a singular conviction: genuine well-being flourishes when plants are respected in their purest form.

Single-Origin Terroir

Our botanical leaves and herbs are nurtured in pristine high-altitude microclimates, where mineral-rich soil and natural rainfall cultivate unmatched therapeutic potency.

Slow Steam Distillation

We extract essential distillates through gentle, low-heat copper steam alembics, capturing pure volatile aromatic compounds without harsh chemical solvents or artificial additives.

Regenerative Care

Every batch supports local farmer cooperatives practicing regenerative agriculture, safeguarding soil vitality and ensuring fair prosperity for indigenous harvesting communities.

Our Guiding Promise

Purity you can taste, aroma that restores calm.

From whole leaf infusion cuts to unadulterated cold-pressed distillates, every formulation is batch-tested for therapeutic integrity. We believe daily wellness should not be an elaborate ritual, but a quiet moment of botanical serenity.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our products and order process.

How should I store the botanical teas and oils?

Keep green teas in an airtight container away from moisture and heat. Essential oils should be kept in a cool, dry place out of direct sunlight to preserve aromatic potency.

What are your shipping timeframes?

Orders are processed within 24 to 48 business hours. Standard domestic delivery takes 3 to 5 business days, shipped in eco-conscious recycled packaging.

What is your return & exchange policy?

We honor 14-day hassle-free returns for unopened items in original packaging. If you receive a damaged blend, reach out to our team immediately for a replacement.
